Proposed governance model

  • Invitation only. Every participant joins with a code issued by the IIMK Admissions Office, which can revoke it at any time.
  • IIMK decides. Modules, content, moderation and rollout stay with the Institute.
  • Consent first. Participants choose what is visible. No phone numbers or email addresses are shown.
  • Minimal data. Name, role, city, industry, session, and what a person offers or seeks.
  • Reviewed content. Opportunities appear only after EPGP Office approval.
  • Institute-controlled data. Hosting, retention and deletion follow the decisions of IIMK’s technical team.
